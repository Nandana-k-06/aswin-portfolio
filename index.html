About me

Good design should feel inevitable, as if it could never have looked any other way.

My work begins with listening. Before I open a single application, I want to understand who a brand is talking to, what it wants them to feel, and what it must never be mistaken for.

I treat every project as a story with a beginning, a turn and a clear ending. Type, colour and composition are the tools I use to make that story easy to follow, whether it is a poster seen from across the street or a label read in a shop aisle.
